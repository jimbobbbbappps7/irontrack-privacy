Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app → Profile → Delete account. This removes your Firebase user and every workout, routine, weight log, and body-stat entry immediately.

How do I switch between kg and lb?
Profile → Units. The whole app flips between metric and imperial in one tap.

Can I sync between devices?
Yes — sign in with the same email/password on any iPhone and everything is there.
