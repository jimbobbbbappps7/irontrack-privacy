# IronTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

## Who we are

IronTrack is a personal fitness logging app. This policy explains what data the
app stores, why, and how you can remove it.

Contact: **jimbobbbbapps7@outlook.com**

## What we store

When you use IronTrack, we store on our servers only what you enter:

- Your chosen username and avatar colour.
- Body stats you log (weight, body fat, measurements, custom fields).
- Workouts you record (exercises, sets, reps, weight, notes, duration).
- Routines you build.
- Any workout video you post to the Feed and any messages you send in chat.
- Feedback you submit.

We do **not** require an email, phone number, or password. No location,
contacts, or ad-tracking data is collected.

## How we use your data

Your data is only used to power features you can see in the app: charts,
history, PR badges, the social feed, direct messages, and feedback. We do not
sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for profiling.

## Third parties

We use Apple / Google platform services only to display the app (fonts, in-app
browser, media picker). IronTrack does not include third-party analytics, ad
SDKs, or tracking.

## How to delete your data

Open **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile and
every record tied to it (workouts, weight logs, body stats, routines, posts,
comments, messages, feedback) from our servers. There is no recovery period.

Alternatively, email **jimbobbbbapps7@outlook.com** with your in-app username
and we will delete your data within 30 days.

## User-generated content

Posts, comments and direct messages are user-generated. IronTrack does not
tolerate abusive, obscene, or unlawful content. You can Report or Block any
user from the Feed or from any DM thread. Reported content is reviewed and, if
it violates our rules, removed within 24 hours.

## Children

IronTrack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13,
contact us at **jimbobbbbapps7@outlook.com** and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your data, we'll update the "Last
updated" date at the top of this page and (where practical) notify you in-app.
